Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Pogo ("the app") is made by Eray Tolun ("we"). This policy explains what the app uses and why. In short: your training data stays on your phone. There are no accounts, no ads, and we never sell data.

What stays on your phone

  • Your answers — goal, race, run days, how far you can run, injuries and similar — used to build and adjust your training plan.
  • Apple Health (with your permission): running workouts, their routes and distance, heart rate, resting heart rate, VO2 max and birth date. We read these to show runs from your watch or another app, check them against your plan and estimate your fitness. The app does not write to Apple Health.
  • Location (with your permission) while you record a run in the app, to measure distance, pace and your route. It keeps working with the screen off during a run so the recording isn't interrupted.
  • Runs, plan, records, races and gear you record or enter in the app.
  • Notifications (with your permission) for training-day and race reminders. They are scheduled on your phone.

None of this is sent to us. Health data is never used for advertising or marketing and is never shared with third parties.

What leaves your phone

  • Finding places to run: when you use Explore, the area you're looking at or the name you search for is sent to Apple Maps, Google Maps and OpenStreetMap's public servers to find places and show their photos. Your identity is not sent.
  • Telling us about a place: after your first run at a place whose access we aren't sure of, we may ask whether you could run there. If you answer, the place, your answer, any note you type and the app version are sent to us (stored on Google Firebase and delivered to us through Telegram). Nothing that identifies you is included. The number of runners who got in is shown to everyone; notes are shown only after we have checked them. Please don't type personal information in the note.
  • Sharing: images of your runs are made on your phone and only go where you send them. The neighbourhood name on the image comes from Apple's map service.
  • Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We don't receive your payment details.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete all my data removes everything the app stored. Deleting the app does the same. Runs saved in Apple Health belong to you and stay there; you can manage them in the Health app. You can withdraw Health, location or notification permission at any time in the iPhone Settings app. To have a place answer you sent removed, write to us at the address below.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Health notice

The app gives general training guidance and is not a medical device or medical advice. Check with a doctor before starting a training programme if you have health concerns.

Changes

If this policy changes — for example if we add backup or anonymous usage statistics — we will update this page and the date above.
